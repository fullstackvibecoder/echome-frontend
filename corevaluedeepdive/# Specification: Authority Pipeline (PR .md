# Specification: Authority Pipeline (PR & Earned Media)

## 1. Evergreen Expertise Extraction
- [cite_start]**Logic**: Identify 2-3 minute segments in videos that are **Evergreen** (relevant in 5-10 years) rather than "Market Updates"[cite: 2272, 2273].
- [cite_start]**Topics**: Look for hacks, family dynamics, stress management, or niche-specific tactics[cite: 2274, 2281].

## 2. The Podcast Pitch Kit
[cite_start]**Output for high-intent/affluent audiences ($150k+ segments)**[cite: 2295]:
- [cite_start]**Topic Hook**: A specific expertise segment[cite: 2307, 2308].
- [cite_start]**Bio**: Abstracted brand story (Heart -> Tension -> Resolution -> Value)[cite: 1712, 1716, 1720].
- [cite_start]**The Reach Out**: A DM draft offering a "Gift" (a training segment) rather than a "Pitch"[cite: 840, 899, 2330].

## 3. High-Impact Nurture
- [cite_start]**Constraint**: Content must be "bite-size" for cold leads who take ~7 months to build trust[cite: 646, 660].
- [cite_start]**Variety**: Enforce a rotation of backgrounds and outfits to prevent "scrolling fatigue"[cite: 661, 2634].
