## 2. The 9-Point Prompting Sequence

1. [cite_start]**Pain**: Use sensory-specific language to describe the current struggle[cite: 2018, 2021].
2. [cite_start]**Problem**: Contrast the "Headache" (what the user thinks) with the "Blood Clot" (what the expert knows)[cite: 2026, 2028, 2031].
3. [cite_start]**Consequences**: Illustrate the "Nightmare Scenario" of inaction (e.g., stress, illness, loss of golden years)[cite: 1671, 1676, 2051].
4. [cite_start]**The Expert Story**: Briefly introduce the `DomainExpert`'s qualifications[cite: 2071, 2076].
5. [cite_start]**SignatureMethod**: Introduce the named process (e.g., "The Tranquility Blueprint")[cite: 1061, 2077].
6. [cite_start]**Proof**: Incorporate data-driven evidence or testimonials[cite: 1729, 2085].
7. [cite_start]**Objection Handling**: Proactively counter "I don't have time" or "I've tried this before"[cite: 1810, 1815, 2091].
8. [cite_start]**Vision**: Describe the sensory-specific future (e.g., sitting on a beach in Tahiti)[cite: 1689, 2100].
