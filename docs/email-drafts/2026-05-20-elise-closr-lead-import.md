Hi Elise,

Yes, this is exactly what Closr is built for. Capturing the people you meet without it turning into a data-entry job is one of the original problems we set out to solve.

A few of the ways Closr handles it today:

1. **CSV import** with smart column mapping. If he's got contacts in a spreadsheet anywhere, drop the file in and Closr maps the fields automatically. Up to 10k+ contacts in a single import, with dedupe.
2. **Follow Up Boss import.** Native, with tags, stages, and notes preserved. If he's been in FUB and wants to move (or just mirror), it's one flow.
3. **Paste a list.** For when contacts are just sitting in a text file or a note, no formatting required.
4. **Phone contacts.** On mobile, Closr can pull from his phone's contact book directly.
5. **Email forwarding.** Forward emails from new contacts and Closr extracts the person and adds them. Useful for the "I just met someone at a showing" case.
6. **Cloe (the in-app chat).** He can literally drop a CSV into the chat and Cloe will preview it, auto-map columns, and import it for him. It's the fastest path if he's not in the mood to learn another interface.

The point of all of these is the same: he shouldn't have to be the one typing names into a CRM. Closr is supposed to absorb the work.
