**The short version**

EchoMe never asks for permission to a folder. We do not access your photos or any cloud storage. When you upload an image, your browser opens the standard file picker, you pick one file, and that file goes to our servers. Nothing else on your device gets touched.

**Where to upload photos on a carousel**

Open any content kit, click on the Carousel section to open the editor, click any slide on the cover or last position, and on the right panel you'll see an "Upload your own" tile at the top of the photo picker. Click it, pick an image (JPG, PNG, or WebP, up to 10MB), and the slide updates.

Cover and last slides accept custom photos. Body slides use the template style on purpose so the deck stays cohesive.
