## Email body

---

Hi {first_name},

There is a specific kind of Sunday-night dread reserved for content. Open the doc. See the cursor. Close the doc. Decide tomorrow.

The fix isn't more buttons. It's less starting from zero.

This month EchoMe got a few moves better at exactly that. Here's what changed, framed as what it can now do with what it already knows about you.

---

### It now reads your kit the way your audience will

When you open a content kit, your outputs are grouped by **what they are to the reader**, not by what we built. Visual on top (clips, carousels, reels). Written below (Substack, platform posts). Everything one click away from the voice it was anchored in.

You scan the way someone scrolling Instagram scans. The kit either looks like you or it doesn't, and you find that out in seconds instead of hunting through tabs.

[Screenshot: Content Kit Detail page — Visual + Written sections]

---

### If you didn't film this week, it has a B-Roll reel ready

You record once a week if you're lucky. The other six days, the algorithm still wants something. So it now pulls a hook from your KB, drops it over B-Roll, and has a 60-second reel ready before lunch.

You pick the B-Roll clip. You pick the text style. You either accept the hook it suggested or write your own. The phone preview shows you exactly what posts.

This is the move that closes the gap between "I know I should be shipping more" and "I have something to ship right now."

[Screenshot: B-Roll Reel Editor — phone preview + segment editor]

---

### Long-form that sounds like you talking, not like a press release

The blog output is gone. In its place, a Substack format that writes the way you'd actually talk to someone over coffee. First-person. Short paragraphs. The bold takeaways where they should be, not sprinkled to look professional.

Hit **Copy for Substack** and paste. Headers, bold, blockquotes all carry over. You're publishing in two clicks, not editing for ten minutes first.

[Screenshot: Written Content modal]

---

### One idea, every platform, one editor

Your LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter, Email, TikTok, and YouTube versions of the same idea now live in one tabbed editor on the kit page. Edit one. Save. Move to the next.

It already adjusted the voice for each platform. You're checking the work, not redoing it.

---

### Zoom passcodes work now

If your team Zooms with a password, it asks for the passcode upfront and handles the rest. Quiet fix. Worth mentioning because it kept tripping up the people who use Zoom for client calls.

---

If you've recorded a video this week, drop it in. The kit it builds will show you which of these moves lands hardest for your voice.

Anything feels off, reply to this email. I read everything.

Ara

**Subject line options:**

**Email body:**

---

Hi {first_name},

We've been heads-down building, and this week's updates are some of our biggest yet. Here's what's new in EchoMe.

---

### Your Content Kit page got a makeover

When you open a content kit, you'll now see your outputs organized into two clear sections — **Visual Content** (clips, carousels, reels) and **Written Content** (Substack articles and platform posts).

Every card has been redesigned with better thumbnails, platform icons, and a cleaner layout. Less clutter, more clarity. You see what you made and you know what to do with it.

[Screenshot: Content Kit Detail page — clips grid + Substack + platform tabs]

---

### B-Roll Reel Maker

This is a brand new output. EchoMe now generates a **short-form B-Roll reel** for every content kit — complete with text overlays that tell a story in 2-4 beats.

Choose from 6 text styles (Bold Impact, Story Cards, Neon, and more), pick your B-Roll clip from our curated library, and adjust text size to your liking. The live phone preview shows you exactly what your audience will see.

You can write your own overlay text or let EchoMe generate authority-level hooks automatically.

[Screenshot: B-Roll Reel Editor — phone preview + segment editor + style picker]

---

### Substack-ready articles

We've replaced our blog output with a dedicated **Substack article** format. The writing is conversational, first-person, and formatted for the way Substack readers actually read — short paragraphs, bold takeaways, and a personal voice throughout.

Hit **Copy for Substack** and paste directly into your Substack editor. Formatting carries over — headers, bold text, blockquotes, all of it.

[Screenshot: Written Content modal — platform tabs + copy button]

---

### Platform posts, all in one place

No more clicking through 6 different cards. Your LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter/X, Email, TikTok, and YouTube posts now live in a single **tabbed editor** right on the content kit page.

Edit, save, regenerate, or copy — all without opening a separate window.

---

### Zoom recording support (with passwords)

If your Zoom recording has a passcode, EchoMe now asks for it upfront and handles the rest. No more failed uploads on password-protected recordings.

---

These updates are live now. Open any content kit to see the new layout, or create a new one to try the B-Roll Reel Maker.

Questions or feedback? Just reply to this email.

— Ara
