# EchoMe Product Walkthrough Script

**Target length:** 8-12 minutes
**Tone:** Conversational, founder-to-user. Not salesy. Show, don't pitch.
**Format:** Screen recording with voiceover. Face cam optional (corner).

---

## INTRO (30 seconds)

> Hey, I'm Ara. I'm going to walk you through EchoMe end to end so you can see exactly how it works and what it can do for your content.
>
> The short version: you give EchoMe one input — a video, a link, a topic, whatever you've got — and it gives you a full week of content. Clips, carousels, captions, articles, reels. All in your voice, not generic AI voice. Let me show you.

---

## CREATE PAGE (1 minute)

*Navigate to /app*

> This is the Create page. One input. That's it.
>
> You can paste a YouTube link, drop a video file, type a topic, or even record a voice note. EchoMe figures out what you gave it and turns it into content.
>
> Let me paste a YouTube link from a recent video I did.

*Paste a YouTube URL, click submit*

> That's it. EchoMe is now downloading the video, transcribing it, analyzing it, and generating content. This usually takes about 60 seconds for a 10-minute video.

---

## CONTENT KIT LIST (30 seconds)

*Navigate to /app/content-kit*

> Everything you create lives here in Content Kits. They're organized by status — Ready to Publish, Processing, Earlier. Each card shows you the thumbnail and how many posts were generated.
>
> Let me open one that's already done.

*Click into a completed content kit*

---

## CONTENT KIT DETAIL — VISUAL CONTENT (2 minutes)

*Show the Visual Content section*

> When you open a content kit, you see two sections. Visual Content up top, Written Content below.
>
> Visual Content is your clips, your carousel, and your B-Roll reel. Let me click into a clip.

### Clip Editor

*Click a clip card to open ClipEditorModal*

> This is the Clip Editor. On the left, your video plays. On the right, you've got controls.
>
> First thing you'll notice — there's a View toggle. Single Speaker shows just you. Split Screen stacks both speakers if you had a conversation. Same clip, two formats.

*Toggle between Single Speaker and Split Screen*

> Below that, Caption Style. You've got options — Color Pop, Modern, Karaoke, Word by Word. Pick the one that matches your brand.

*Switch between a couple caption styles*

> And positioning — top, center, or bottom. When you're happy, hit Download 1080p. That's your ready-to-post clip with captions burned in.

*Close the clip editor*

### Carousel

*Click the Carousel card*

> This is the Carousel Editor. EchoMe generated these slides from your content automatically. You can edit the text on any slide right here.
>
> And you've got style options. Quote Card gives you that X/Twitter post look. Text on Color, My Image if you want your own background, or Video Frame which uses a frame from your video.

*Switch between styles*

> Download individual slides or grab them all at once.

*Close carousel editor*

### B-Roll Reel

*Click the B-Roll Reel card*

> The Reel Editor. Pick a B-Roll clip from the library — we've got categories like Real Estate, Lifestyle, Abstract. Or upload your own with the plus button.
>
> Your text overlays go on top. You can type your own or let EchoMe generate authority hooks. There are 6 text styles to choose from.
>
> Adjust the text size, hit Generate Reel, and you've got a ready-to-post reel.

*Close reel editor*

---

## CONTENT KIT DETAIL — WRITTEN CONTENT (1.5 minutes)

*Scroll down to Written Content section*

### Substack Article

*Click the Substack card*

> EchoMe generates a full Substack article from your content. Not a summary — a real article with sections, takeaways, and a personal voice.
>
> You can generate a header image right here. It creates a custom illustration in our signature style. Download it for your Substack header.
>
> Edit any section, then Copy for Substack. It copies with formatting — bold, headers, blockquotes — so when you paste into Substack, it looks right.

*Show the Copy for Substack button, close the modal*

### Platform Posts

*Click through the platform tabs*

> Below the article, you've got posts for every platform. LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter/X, Email, TikTok, YouTube.
>
> Each one is written differently. Your LinkedIn post doesn't read like your Instagram caption. EchoMe adapts the format, the tone, the length.
>
> You can edit any post, save it, regenerate if you want a fresh take, or copy it. And there's a Schedule button that adds it to your content calendar as a reminder.

*Show the Schedule button, briefly show the modal*

---

## BUILD YOUR VOICE (1.5 minutes)

*Navigate to /app/knowledge*

> This is Build Your Voice. This is how EchoMe learns to sound like you.
>
> One input. Paste your best writing, drop a PDF, paste a YouTube link from your channel, record a voice note. Whatever shows how you communicate — put it here.
>
> The more you add, the stronger your voice match gets. You can see the score up top — it shows how well Echo knows your style.

*Point to the voice strength meter*

> Below the input, Ask Echo. This is where you can talk to your knowledge base. Ask it "how well do you know me" or "give me content ideas." It uses everything you've uploaded to give you answers and inspiration.

*Click one of the suggestion chips*

> And Sources at the bottom shows everything you've added — YouTube imports, voice recordings, documents, social posts. You can manage it all from here.

---

## FOLLOWING (1 minute)

*Navigate to /app/following*

> Following is where content inspiration comes from. Paste a YouTube channel or Instagram profile URL and you'll see their latest content in your feed.
>
> The magic is the Repurpose button. You see a video from a creator you admire, hit Repurpose, and EchoMe takes their idea — not their words — and creates content in YOUR voice about that topic.
>
> You're not copying anyone. You're using their ideas as a starting point and Echo writes it the way you'd say it.

*Show a couple feed items with Repurpose buttons*

---

*Navigate to /app/reels*

> The Reel Maker page shows all your reels organized by status — Drafts, Rendering, Ready. You can start a new reel from a topic, use the B-Roll Wizard for a guided flow, or create one from a Content Kit.

> Draft cards show your hook text as a preview so you can tell them apart at a glance.

## TIPS FOR RECORDING

- Screen record at 1920x1080, clean browser (hide bookmarks bar, close other tabs)
- Have a content kit already generated and ready before recording
- Pre-load a YouTube URL to paste (don't wait for generation on camera — cut to the finished result)
- For the clip editor, have a clip with split screen available
- For Build Your Voice, have some sources already added so the score shows "Strong"
- Talk to the viewer like they're sitting next to you, not like you're reading a script
- If you stumble, just pause and restart that sentence — you can cut in post
