# Hero Showcase Image Mapping

Replace these 5 placeholder files with cropped versions of your screenshots:

## 1. source-video.png
**Source:** Your video reel screenshot with "80% viral" badge
**Crop:** Center crop to show the video frame clearly
**Aspect:** 16:9 (landscape video)
**Used in:** Main video library frame at the top

## 2. instagram-reel.png
**Source:** Instagram or TikTok vertical post screenshot
**Crop:** Vertical portrait orientation, crop to main content area
**Aspect:** 9:16 (portrait)
**Used in:** First output slot (top-left in grid)

## 3. twitter-post.png
**Source:** Twitter/X post screenshot
**Crop:** Square crop showing the tweet content and visual
**Aspect:** 1:1 (square)
**Used in:** Second output slot (top-right in grid)

## 4. linkedin-post.png
**Source:** LinkedIn post screenshot
**Crop:** Tall portrait showing the post content
**Aspect:** 4:5 (Instagram post ratio)
**Used in:** Third output slot (bottom-left in grid)

## 5. newsletter.png
**Source:** Newsletter or Blog post screenshot
**Crop:** Tall crop showing the email/blog header and content
**Aspect:** 3:4 (tall portrait)
**Used in:** Fourth output slot (bottom-right in grid)

---

## Cropping Tips
- Keep text readable - don't crop too tight
- Ensure platform branding is visible if present
- Use high-resolution versions (at least 800px on longest side)
- Export as PNG for best quality
- Remove any sensitive/personal information if needed
